# Tumbleweed TexStyles vs. Agave Apparel's current tee (researched 2026-10-14)

Public info only. Nothing was purchased and no one was contacted. Inferences are marked **[GUESS]**.

## TL;DR
- Almost all of Tumbleweed's tees (152 t-shirt listings) use the **same blank we already have: Bella+Canvas 3001** (4.2 oz, "Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton"). They sell it mostly in **Heather (CVC 52/48 cotton/poly) colors**, not solid 100% cotton colors like our Natural. [1][2][4]
- Their product copy matches Printful's 3001 description word for word, and every tee says it is "made especially for you as soon as you place an order". **[GUESS]** They fulfill on demand through Printful (or something similar) using DTG, so their tees today are probably not screen printed. [1][2][4]
- One tee (Cactus Cowboy) and their long sleeves and sweatshirts use **Comfort Colors** garment-dyed blanks (1717-type 6.1 oz tee, 6.4 oz/9.5 oz crews). [2]
- **The biggest single change toward "the Tumbleweed feel" is switching our 3001 color from Natural (100% cotton) to Heather colors such as Heather Natural, Heather Dust or Heather Clay.** It's the same Printful product and the same price.

## 2. Print method
- Every tee is described as "made especially for you as soon as you place an order… made on demand instead of in bulk" (Printful's standard text). **[GUESS]** This means DTG printing on demand. [1][2]
- They screen printed historically. In a Shopify interview the owners describe their early one-color screen-print runs and later stretching 2 colors to make 3–4. [3] A 2014 blog post calls them screen-print shirts. [5]
- Look: hand-drawn, illustrative Texas art with limited palettes that often use one ink color (e.g. "w/ tan ink", "w/ red ink" variants). [2][3] **[GUESS]** DTG on a heather CVC blend gives a soft, slightly faded/vintage print because the poly fibers don't take the ink.

## 3. Price, design, and praise
- Tees $29.95 (S–XL), $31.95–$33.95 for larger sizes. Comfort Colors long sleeves $34, sweatshirts $52, hats $32–34. [2][6]
- Designs are hand-drawn in house by co-owners Jeb Matulich and Hillary. Themes include Texas pride, towns, wildflowers, flags, music and lake life. [1][3][6]
- Praise: "these shirts are so so soft!… wear to a concert one night and brunch the next weekend" [5]. The brand calls its tees "the softest t-shirts and best quality garments we can find" [6]. I couldn't find public review aggregates or Reddit threads.

## 5. Recommended path
1. **Art:** a hand-drawn-style design exported as vector (AI/SVG/PDF) plus a 300 dpi transparent PNG at roughly 12"×16" for Printful. Use 1–2 ink colors for the Tumbleweed look.
2. **Blank:** duplicate our Printful template on **B+C 3001 in Heather Natural, Heather Dust, Heather Clay and Heather Olive** (desert palette) at the same $11.92 base. Optional premium line: **Comfort Colors 1717** (Ivory, Sand, Moss, Khaki) at $15.60+.
3. **Sample:** order 1–2 samples (≈$12–16 each plus shipping; Printful offers sample discounts). **This costs money and needs Jay's yes.**
4. **Pricing:** retail at $28–30, like Tumbleweed. At about $13 base plus shipping, that leaves roughly $12–15 margin before fees. **[GUESS]**
5. **Later:** once a design sells 24+ units per run, get a quote from a Phoenix screen printer (Designed In Ink or Eyeconic) for a lower per-unit cost and a classic screen-print hand.
